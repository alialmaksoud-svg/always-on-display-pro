Always On Display Pro

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what the Always On Display Pro app ("the app", "we") does with information on your phone. In short: everything stays on your phone. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

Summary

  • Your notifications are read on your phone only, to show them on the always-on display. They are never uploaded, stored on a server or shared.
  • The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Information the app uses

1. Notifications (Notification access)

With your permission, the app uses Android's notification listener to read the notifications on your phone: the app name and icon, the title, the message text and the time. It uses them only to draw them on its always-on display and lock-screen cards, and to clear them when you swipe them away. This happens entirely on your device. Notification content is kept in memory while it is shown and is not written to a server, sent over the internet, logged or shared with anyone.

You can choose to hide message content, turn off individual apps, or revoke Notification access at any time in Android Settings.

2. Accessibility service

The app includes an accessibility service. It is used only to draw the always-on display over the lock screen, to turn it on and off with the screen, and to leave an open area over the in-display fingerprint sensor so that unlocking keeps working. The service does not read, record or collect the content of other apps' screens, what you type, or your passwords. It does not perform actions in other apps on your behalf. You can turn it off at any time in Settings › Accessibility.

3. Wallpaper and photos

To show your wallpaper behind the clock, the app either reads your phone's wallpaper (only in the version distributed outside Google Play, and only if you grant the "All files access" permission for that purpose) or uses a photo you pick yourself with Android's photo picker. The image is used only for display and a copy is kept only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded.

4. Settings

Your preferences (clock style, card size, colours, brightness, which apps are shown, phone model, and similar) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.

5. Device information

The app reads your phone's brand and model on the device, only to pre-select the right layout and fingerprint position. It is not sent anywhere.

Subscriptions and payments

Paid features are offered as monthly and yearly subscriptions through Google Play Billing. New subscribers may receive a 5-day free trial. Google processes all payments and stores your payment information under Google's own privacy policy. We receive no card details, billing address or other payment information. The app only receives from Google Play whether your subscription is active, so it can unlock the paid features.

Update checks (version outside Google Play only)

The version of the app distributed outside Google Play may check the app's public GitHub release page for a newer version. This request contains no personal data; like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to GitHub, which handles it under GitHub's privacy policy. The Google Play version does not do this; it is updated by Google Play.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, rent or share personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles.
  • We do not transfer your data to any server, because the app has none.

Permissions

The app asks only for permissions it needs for the features above: Notification access, the accessibility service, display over the lock screen, wake lock, notifications (for update and test messages), and, outside Google Play only, All files access (wallpaper) and installing updates. Each one can be revoked in Android Settings; the related feature then stops working.

Data retention and deletion

All data the app uses stays on your phone. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings › Apps › Always On Display Pro › Storage, or by uninstalling the app, which removes everything it stored. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The app does not collect personal information from anyone.

Security

Keeping data on your phone, inside the app's private storage, is the main protection: there is no server to breach. Your phone's own lock (PIN, pattern, fingerprint or face) continues to protect your device; the app does not replace or bypass it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since the app does not collect personal data off your device, these rights are exercised directly on your phone as described above. You can still contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
